The Venue

Rooms worth showing up for.

Where a conversation happens changes the conversation. We pick our spaces with as much care as the guest list.

The spaces

Three rooms, three moods.

Each one is set up for a particular kind of evening and produced by our team from the first candle to the last car.

The Table Room

A single long table under low, warm light. Built for suppers where everyone can hear everyone and nobody is stuck at the end.

Seats 24SuppersBoard dinners

The Terrace

Open air, facing west, timed to the sunset. Fire pits for the cooler months and a long bar for the nightcap that always runs late.

Up to 80Sunset SessionsLaunches

The Study

Quiet, enclosed and private. A whiteboard wall, good coffee and a door that closes. Home of the Deal Room and confidential sessions.

Point Loma

On the water: sailing mornings and harbour suppers.

Anza-Borrego

Dark skies and open space for the annual retreat.
