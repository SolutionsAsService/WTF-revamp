The Circle

A small room of people who build.

We keep the circle small on purpose. That way you know everyone at the table, and they know you.

Who it's for

You've built something. Now what?

The circle is for people who are past the beginning: founders with traction, operators running teams, and investors who write real cheques. Industry doesn't matter. Biotech sits next to hospitality, defence tech next to a surf brand.

What matters is that you're building something in Southern California, you're generous with what you know, and you would rather have one honest conversation than fifty business cards.

We don't publish a member list and we never will. Who's in the room stays in the room.

Ways in

Three ways to sit down.

Everyone starts as a guest. If it's a fit for both sides, the circle grows by one.

Guest

A seat for the night

Invited by a member or by us to a single Long Table or Sunset Session. The best way to see whether this is your room.

Member

A standing place

Priority seats at every gathering, a turn in the Deal Room, the annual retreat, the members' channel and the ability to bring guests.

Host

Your own table

Members who use the venue for their own board dinners, offsites and launches, produced end to end by our team.

House rules

Few rules, firmly kept.

These rules are why people tell the truth at our tables.

  1. What's said here, stays here.

    Chatham House at every table. Share what you learned, never who said it.

  2. Give before you ask.

    Intros, advice, a second opinion. Every member is expected to put more into the circle than they take out.

  3. No pitching at the table.

    If someone wants to hear about your round, they'll ask. Usually over the nightcap.

  4. Phones face down.

    The point is to be in the room. Photos only when we say the cameras are on.

  5. Show up.

    Seats are scarce. Confirm, come, and if life happens, tell us early so someone else can sit down.

The path

From request to regular.

Start with step one →

  1. Request or nomination

    Tell us about yourself, or a member puts your name forward. Nominations go to the top of the pile.

  2. A coffee

    Thirty minutes with one of our hosts. We want to understand what you're building and who you'd want to meet.

  3. A first seat

    You join a Long Table or Sunset Session as a guest. No obligations and no hard sell.

  4. Welcome in

    If it's right for both sides, you're invited to join and your host introduces you to the circle.

Questions

Fair questions.

Do I need to be a founder?

No. We look for people who build: founders, operators, investors and makers. Titles matter less than whether you'd make the table better.

Is there a revenue or funding threshold?

There's no hard number. Most members are past their first real proof point, whether that's revenue, a team, a fund or a body of work. We'd rather meet you and decide than filter you out on a spreadsheet.

What does membership cost?

Membership is an annual commitment that covers every gathering, the retreat and member hosting rates. We share the details over coffee, once we both know it's a fit.

I don't live in San Diego. Can I still join?

Most of the circle lives between Oceanside and Chula Vista, but some members travel in from Orange County, LA and further away. If you'll be at the table regularly, you're welcome.

Is this a deal-flow club?

Deals happen, but they come from trust. If you're mostly looking for deal flow, there are better rooms for that. If you want the people behind the deals, this is the one.
