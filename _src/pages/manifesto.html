Manifesto

Yes, it spells WTF.

That's on purpose. Building something is mostly a series of "what the…" moments, and it helps to have people to share them with.

The name

Three words, one idea.

W

Whitestone

A cornerstone: something solid to build on. The circle is meant to be the foundation you come back to while everything else is moving.

T

Taylored

Made to fit, not off the rack. Every guest list, seating plan and menu is built around the people in the room.

F

Functions

Gatherings with a purpose. We started out producing events, and we still believe a well-made evening can change the course of a company.

What we believe

Building is lonely. The higher you climb, the fewer people you can be honest with.

Rooms matter. A good table, the right light and a slow dinner make people say what they actually think.

Small beats big. Twenty-four people you know are worth more than two thousand you've met.

San Diego is ready. The talent and the capital are already here. They just need a table to share.

Where we came from

From producing events to building a circle.

Whitestone Taylored Functions began as an event production company: venues, sound, light, film and all the details nobody notices when they're done well.

Along the way we noticed something. The best part of every event we produced was never the stage. It was the half hour afterwards, when the right few people ended up at the same table.

So we rebuilt WTF around that half hour. We keep the same craft and care, but now the guest list is the point.
