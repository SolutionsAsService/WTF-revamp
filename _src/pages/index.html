The idea

Most networking is loud and shallow. We built the opposite: a small circle of people who build things, meeting in rooms worth showing up for.

San Diego has no shortage of founders, operators and capital. What it lacks is a place where they actually talk: candidly, with no badges, no pitch decks and no one working the room.

WTF is that place. We curate the people, we design the evenings, and we take care of every detail so the only thing you have to bring is yourself.

Anatomy of an evening

How a Long Table runs.

Every gathering is designed, not just booked. Here is the shape of a typical monthly supper.

  1. 6:30

    Golden hour

    Arrive as the sun drops. A drink on the terrace and quiet introductions from your host, who has already read up on you.

  2. 7:15

    The table

    Twenty-four seats with a seating plan built around who should meet. Three courses from a chef we love.

  3. 8:30

    The hot seat

    One member brings a real problem they're stuck on: hiring, a raise, an exit. The table works on it together.

  4. 9:45

    Nightcap

    Back outside for the conversations that started at dinner. Most of the deals and friendships start here.
