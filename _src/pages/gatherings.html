Gatherings

Every evening, designed.

Five formats, each built for a different kind of conversation. None of them involve a lanyard.

Monthly · Supper

The Long Table

24 seatsMembers + guestsEvening

Our signature evening. Twenty-four people, one long table and a seating plan we spend more time on than the menu. It opens at golden hour on the terrace and ends with the hot seat: one member brings a real problem, and the table works on it.

  • Three courses from a rotating San Diego chef
  • Seating curated around who should meet
  • The hot seat: one problem, twenty-three minds

Quarterly · Terrace

Sunset Sessions

60 guestsMembers bring twoGolden hour

Our widest circle. Members each bring two people they think the room should know. Live music, a raw bar and the Pacific going orange. It's the easiest way to meet the circle and the best way to bring a co-founder into it.

  • Open-air on the coast, timed to the sunset
  • Short, sharp fireside with one guest of honour
  • Where most new members first sit down

Monthly · Closed door

The Deal Room

8 seatsMembers onlyMorning

Eight people, two hours and a whiteboard. One member presents a live decision, such as a term sheet, an acquisition offer, a co-founder split or a pivot, and the room pressure-tests it. It's the most candid two hours in the county.

  • Strict confidentiality, no notes leave the room
  • Mix of operators and investors at every session
  • Members request a slot; we build the room

Seasonal · Outdoors

Field Days

12 to 20MembersSunrise

Some conversations go better side by side than face to face. A dawn surf session at Black's, a morning on the water out of Point Loma, a round on the coast or a hike above Torrey Pines, followed by a long breakfast.

  • Instruction and gear arranged for every level
  • Small groups, no agenda
  • Breakfast is the meeting

Annual · Three days

The Desert Retreat

30 membersAnza-BorregoSpring

Once a year we leave the coast for the desert. Three days of long walks, longer dinners, dark-sky nights and structured sessions on the questions nobody has time for in the city: what you're building, why, and what comes after.

  • Accommodation, meals and production handled
  • Guided sessions plus plenty of open time
  • The circle's favourite three days of the year
